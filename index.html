Program Structure

NTT Course Curriculum

A structured one-year program designed to build complete competency in early childhood education.

01

Child Growth & Development

Understanding developmental milestones, learning theories, and age-appropriate pedagogy for 0–6 years.

02

Psychology of Early Learning

Cognitive, emotional, social development; Piaget, Vygotsky, and play-based learning frameworks.

03

Activity & Lesson Planning

Designing theme-based lesson plans, craft activities, storytelling sessions, and daily classroom routines.

04

Health, Nutrition & Safety

Child health basics, first aid, nutrition for toddlers, and creating safe learning environments.

05

Language & Communication

Early literacy strategies, bilingual classrooms, storytelling, phonics, and parent communication.

06

Practicum & Internship

Supervised teaching practice at affiliated preschools with mentored feedback and evaluation.

Assessment

Final Examination & Certification

Final examinations are held in May every year. Candidates must strictly follow the rules and regulations outlined in the Prospectus.

  • The NTT certification is highly valued for pre-primary, private, and public schools.
  • Our training develops self-confidence, making you fully equipped to open and run your own Nursery School.
  • Successful students are issued an official Mark-Sheet and Certificate upon qualifying.
